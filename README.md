# upgrade

A map of opportunities to study, work and build abroad for an Indonesian software / AI / robotics engineer and founder: scholarships, fellowships, research internships, talent visas, working holidays, remote work, startup programs and grants, including the obscure ones.

**Live page:** https://altamisatmaja.github.io/upgrade/

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Simple page to search and filter everything |
| `DATABASE.md` | Every entry grouped by category, best fit first |
| `opportunities.csv` | Same data for Excel / Google Sheets |
| `data.json` | Same data for the page |
| `data.txt` | **Source of truth.** Edit this, then run `python3 build.py` |
| `automation/` | Weekly local job that researches new opportunities and re-verifies old ones |

**Status:** ✅ = verified on the web (date in `checked_on`). ⚠️ = from general knowledge, **re-check the official link before applying**. Some rows bundle several programs, so the real count is higher than the row count.

## 2. Best fits for a software / AI / robotics engineer and founder

**Study with no return bond (you can stay and work afterwards):**
1. **Erasmus Mundus robotics/AI**: EU4M, JEMARO, SpaceMaster, SE4GD. Fully covered, study in 2-3 countries, European network.
2. **MBZUAI / KAUST**: well-funded universities, large stipends, strong AI and robotics. Deadlines Dec-Jan.
3. **Funded research master's/PhD** (KAIST, UST-KIST, OIST, ETH, US/Canada PhD): not a "scholarship" but a *job* as a research assistant. Email professors with a real project as proof.
4. **MEXT (Japan)** via the Embassy or university track.

**Money and global experience without moving yet:**
5. Remote jobs: Arc.dev, Wellfound, YC Work at a Startup, HN "Who is hiring", remote-first companies. Remote developers in Indonesia average around US$48-50k/year (Plane & Arc data).
6. Mercor/micro1 (AI training work): USD bridge income.
7. LFX Mentorship (CNCF, Go-friendly), GSoC, Algora bounties: international portfolio + pay.

**Founder track (eldercare / robotics / AI products):**
8. **MIT Solve, AgeTech Collaborative, UNICEF Venture Fund, Echoing Green, YSEALI Seeds**: all relevant for eldercare robotics.
9. **Emergent Ventures**: rolling grants with a short application, made for unusual builders.
10. K-Startup Grand Challenge, Antler, Entrepreneur First, YC.

## 3. Things most people don't know

1. **Many big scholarships come with a return bond.** LPDP (serve 2n+1 years), Chevening (2 years), Australia Awards (2 years), Fulbright (J-1 = 2 years in Indonesia). If the goal is to *live and work* abroad, prefer bond-free options: Erasmus Mundus, MEXT, KAUST, MBZUAI, SINGA, ETH, or paid research degrees. Check each program's rules.
2. **A top-100 university unlocks talent visas.** Graduates of top universities can use Japan J-Find (2 years to job hunt), UK HPI (2 years without a sponsor), Hong Kong TTPS and the Dutch Zoekjaar. Pick a master's school partly by *which visas open up after graduating*.
3. **The Australia Working Holiday cap for 2026-27 is full** (5,000 places, filled in August). Australia and Indonesia agreed to move to a **ballot**, no date yet. The SDUWHV letter is only dropped once the ballot runs. The visa fee rose to AUD 840. **The age limit is 30 at application**, so prioritise this if you are close to 30. Prepare now: functional English (around IELTS 4.5), degree, around AUD 5,000 in funds.
4. **A New Zealand-Indonesia Working Holiday** is listed as "In Progress" in NZ's treaty database. If it goes live, it becomes a second WHV for Indonesians.
5. **Japan now asks for JLPT N2** for the Engineer visa since 15 Apr 2026. Sources disagree on whether it applies only to Japanese-speaking roles. The safe route to Japan: a master's (MEXT) first, while learning Japanese.
6. **Chevening counts internships, freelance and part-time work** towards its 2,800-hour requirement.
7. **Remote job → nomad visa → abroad without a scholarship.** With a remote job paying ≥ US$24k/year you can live in Malaysia (DE Rantau). With enough savings there is Thailand's DTV (5 years). Taiwan's Gold Card (around NT$160k/month salary) lets you work for anyone from Taiwan.
8. **Tax on remote income:** as an Indonesian tax resident, foreign income still has to be reported in your annual SPT. Get an NPWP, a USD-receiving account (Wise/Payoneer), and learn how Deel/Remote pay Indonesian contractors.
9. **Erasmus Mundus can only be awarded once per lifetime.** Don't spend it on a program you are lukewarm about.
10. **A real, public project beats any essay.** Open-source your best project with a technical write-up and a demo video. It is concrete proof for robotics essays, research applications, MIT Solve, Emergent Ventures and remote job interviews.
11. **Beware of fake job/WHV agents.** Official overseas job placements go through KP2MI. The WHV is applied for yourself, without an agent.

## 4. Preparation checklist

**Now - December 2026**
- [ ] English test: aim for **IELTS 7.0 (every band ≥ 6.5)** or TOEFL iBT 95+. One test is valid 2 years and covers almost every program
- [ ] Line up **3 referees** (academic supervisor, internship manager, client/partner). Ask early
- [ ] Documents: passport (valid ≥ 2 years), diploma & transcript + sworn translations, European/US-style CV
- [ ] Open-source your best project + write one technical article
- [ ] Pick 3-5 main targets from section 2; send MBZUAI (15 Dec) and KAUST (3 Jan) if interested

**January - March 2027**
- [ ] Erasmus Mundus (Jan), SI (Feb), Fulbright (Feb), ICDF (Mar)
- [ ] Essay bank: motivation, leadership, career plan, impact on Indonesia. Write once, adapt many times
- [ ] Start applying for remote jobs and LFX/GSoC

**April - July 2027**
- [ ] MEXT, GKS, AAS, Manaaki, KSGC, LPDP
- [ ] Watch the Australia WHV ballot
